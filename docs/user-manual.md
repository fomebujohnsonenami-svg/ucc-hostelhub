# User manual

## Public browsing

Open **Hostels** to search by name or area. Filter by price range, room type, amenities and reported availability. Switch between Grid, List and Map. Map View uses the filtered page and only plots listings with saved coordinates. Open a listing to see photos, room rates and the last availability confirmation. **Get Directions** opens Google Maps in a new tab when coordinates are present.

## Students

Sign in or register, then complete a student profile. Save a hostel by selecting its heart. Find it later in **Saved Hostels**. For an inquiry, open the listing and choose **Contact Manager**. Open **Find Roommate**, set your preferences and visibility, and choose **Message Roommate** to write to another student. Find replies in **Messages**. Potential matches are ranked by budget overlap, preferred area and shared habits.

## Managers

Sign in and complete the manager profile. In **My Hostels**, add listing details, rooms, photos and a map position. Preview a draft there. Submit a listing for review, then publish it after approval. Open **Availability** to confirm the count for the correct room type. Review and reply to inquiries in **Messages**. **Hostel Promotion** lets you feature eligible listings and view inquiry counts. **Upgrade to Premium** opens the Coming Soon dialog.

## Administrators

Sign in with the administrator account provisioned by the operator. Use **Manager Verification** to review managers, **Listing Review** to approve, reject or suspend listings, and **Content Moderation** for reports. **User Management** lists registered profiles.

Availability is reported by managers and can become stale. Confirm it before making plans. The presentation does not process payments or reservations.
